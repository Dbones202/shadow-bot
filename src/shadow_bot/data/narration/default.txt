# Default narration shipped with Shadow Bot.
#
# Format: one line per entry, grouped under [category.outcome] headers.
# Blank lines and lines starting with # are ignored. No quoting or escaping —
# apostrophes and quotes can be written normally.
#
# Placeholders are replaced where known and left visible where not, so a typo
# like {amout} shows up in the message instead of vanishing.
#
#   {user}      the member acting          {amount}  formatted money
#   {target}    the other member           {currency} plural currency name
#   {tribute}   a games participant        {victim}  who they eliminated
#   {winner}    the surviving tribute      {pot}     the prize pot
#
# A guild can override any section with /flavor add; its own lines then replace
# the defaults for that section only.

# =============================================================================
# Activities
# =============================================================================

[work.success]
{user} pulled a double at the diner and made {amount}.
{user} sold hand-drawn portraits outside the station and earned {amount}.
{user} spent the afternoon fixing someone's fence for {amount}.
{user} walked eleven dogs at once and somehow came back with {amount}.
{user} covered a shift nobody wanted and took home {amount}.

[work.failure]
{user} slept through the alarm and was docked {amount}.
{user} put the decimal point in the wrong place. It cost {amount}.
{user} reversed the delivery van into the manager's car. {amount} gone.

[crime.success]
{user} relieved a vending machine of its contents and got away with {amount}.
{user} sold a bridge they did not own. {amount} richer.
{user} ran a very convincing raffle with no prize. {amount}.

[crime.failure]
{user} tripped the alarm on the way out and was fined {amount}.
{user} tried to rob a shop that was already being robbed. Fined {amount}.
{user} left their wallet at the scene. {amount}.

[steal.success]
{user} lifted {amount} straight out of {target}'s pocket.
{user} distracted {target} with a compliment and took {amount}.
{target} really should not have left that bag open. {user} took {amount}.

[steal.failure]
{target} caught {user} mid-reach. {amount} in restitution.
{user} picked the pocket of someone with faster hands. Fined {amount}.

[steal.empty]
{user} got into {target}'s pockets and found nothing but lint.
{target} has nothing worth taking. {user} left disappointed.

[slut.success]
{user} worked the room and came away with {amount}.
{user} charmed a very generous stranger out of {amount}.

[slut.failure]
{user} misread the room entirely. {amount}.
{user} picked the wrong table and paid {amount} for the privilege.

[hungrygames.death]
{tribute} eats the wrong berries.
{tribute} loses an argument with a ravine.
{tribute} builds a fire, which is exactly as clever as it sounds.
{tribute} is taken by something nobody sees clearly.
{tribute} drinks from the wrong stream.
{tribute} falls asleep in a very poorly chosen tree.

[hungrygames.kill]
{tribute} takes out {victim} with a well-aimed rock.
{tribute} sets a snare. {victim} walks straight into it.
{victim} trusts {tribute}. This turns out to be a mistake.
{tribute} and {victim} fight over a tin of peaches. Only {tribute} leaves.
{tribute} eliminates {victim} and does not seem sorry about it.

[hungrygames.survive]
{tribute} finds a river and rests.
{tribute} spends the round hiding in a hedge, and it works.
{tribute} makes camp somewhere sensible for once.
{tribute} finds a crate of supplies and tells absolutely nobody.
{tribute} does nothing memorable and survives anyway.

[hungrygames.winner]
{winner} is the last one standing, and takes {pot}.
It is over. {winner} wins {pot}.
{winner} outlasts everyone and claims {pot}.

# =============================================================================
# Hungry Games — forfeits and rewards
#
# Consequences attached to how someone left the arena. These are social, not
# enforced by the bot — it posts them and the server does the rest.
# =============================================================================

[hungrygames.forfeit_death]
{tribute} must post their most embarrassing photo.
{tribute} owes the server one genuinely terrible joke.
{tribute} has to change their nickname to something the winner picks.
{tribute} must recount how they died, in detail, in voice chat.

[hungrygames.forfeit_kill]
{victim} must publicly congratulate {tribute} on a clean kill.
{victim} owes {tribute} a favour of {tribute}'s choosing.
{victim} has to say something nice about {tribute}. Out loud.

[hungrygames.reward_winner]
{winner} may assign one task to anyone who fell in the arena.
{winner} picks what happens at the next game night.
{winner} gets to rename one loser for the week.
